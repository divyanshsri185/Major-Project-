Take the exam. We keep it fair.

Invigil watches for the things that break exam integrity — leaving the tab, looking away, extra voices, copy and paste — and gives the examiner a clear report afterward.

Video and audio are analysed in your browser and never uploaded. Only the resulting flags (for example “tab switched”) are sent to the examiner.
